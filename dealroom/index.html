What is Kaizan?

Kaizan is the AI Client Success platform for companies that manage valuable, high-touch client relationships, so agents can help them maximise the revenue of every client.

It brings conversations, emails, attachments, chat messages and the work being delivered on each client into one secure, continuously updated client memory so that your team and their agents can proactively manage and grow each client.

Our proprietary AI, CARE™, scores and benchmarks relationships to show the risks, delivery issues and growth opportunities on every client 24/7.

How it works

How it works, step by step

1. One secure client memory

Conversations, emails, messages and the work being delivered, brought into one memory per client. Deduplicated, stakeholders resolved, permissions preserved.

2. Continuously updated

Who said what, what was promised, what was delivered, what the client wants and how that has changed. The memory stays with the firm when people move on.

3. CARE™ scores the relationship

Relationship scoring across 24 dimensions from the real communications, benchmarked against comparable firms. It identifies risks, delivery friction and growth opportunities.

4. Clear actions for the account team

Each signal has a defined next action, for example notifying the account lead when a decision-maker has been inactive for a month, or flagging a scope gap when delivery exceeds the contract. Actions are informed by what works across comparable firms.

5. Agents do the routine work

Briefs, follow-ups, alerts and upsell recommendations are produced automatically. The result is more consistent client service and a lower cost to serve each client.

The pilot and the first 90 days

The pilot runs on your own clients against criteria agreed at the start, with dates agreed together. A decision is expected within a week of the review. From there, a dedicated Account Manager and a tracked 90-day plan.

Proof criteria

Three or four criteria are agreed at kickoff and reported against at the review. Typical examples:

  • Hours per week per Account Manager on prep, follow-ups and reporting, before and after.
  • Commitments and risks surfaced that were not logged anywhere.
  • Revenue gaps identified across the pilot clients, in currency.
  • Follow-up emails sent within one working day, as a percentage.
