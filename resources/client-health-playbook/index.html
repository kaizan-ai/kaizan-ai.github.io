02 · The window

Relationships erode. They rarely snap.

Status updates say a project is on track. The sentiment underneath says otherwise. Between the first sign of drift and a client’s decision to leave, there is a window.

Visibility gives you the opportunity to act while it’s still open.

03 · What’s inside

What the best client teams do differently

Five habits from client service leaders who solved the problem in practice. None of them started with a perfect system. Each comes with the trap that catches teams who adopt it half-heartedly.

1
Centralise every channel into one viewThe goal is a view of the account, not an archive.
2
Standardise the process across every teamStandardise the judgement, not just the report.
3
Reinvest time saved on admin into watchingPoint the freed time at early signals: reduced engagement, vaguer feedback.
4
Watch for underlying sentiment, not statusA polite or collaborative tone is not the same as a safe one.
5
Make “at risk” a shared definition, not tribal knowledgeWrite it down on one page the whole team can act on.
