Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or device by websites that you visit. They are widely used in order to make websites work, or work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site. This policy explains the cookies we use on kaizan.ai (the “Website”) and why.

We only set optional cookies (such as analytics and marketing cookies) if you accept them using the cookie banner shown on your first visit. If you decline, no optional cookies are set. You can change your choice at any time using the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page.

THIRD PARTY COOKIES

Some cookies may be set by third parties when you visit this Website, for example suppliers who partner with us to deliver our Services. These cookies are controlled by the third parties, so please check the third-party websites for more information about these cookies and how to manage them.

HOW DO I CHANGE MY COOKIE SETTINGS

The easiest way is the “Cookie settings” link in the footer of every page, which reopens the banner so you can change your choice. Your choice is stored for 12 months, after which we will ask again.
