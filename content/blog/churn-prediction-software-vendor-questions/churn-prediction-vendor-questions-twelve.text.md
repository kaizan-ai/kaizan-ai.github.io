**Churn prediction software: 12 questions to ask any vendor**

Ask these before a single demo.

**On the data**
1. What data actually feeds the score?
2. Does the model need the client to do anything?
3. Is the signal observed or self-reported?
4. Does a quiet account score as safe, or as a blind spot?

**On the model**
5. What is it calibrated against?
6. How often is the score refreshed, and how often is the model retrained?
7. Can you see the evidence behind a score, or just the number?
8. What happens when the model is wrong?

**On data handling and governance**
9. Is our data ever used to train someone else's model?
10. What certifications and audits back that up?
11. Is every model version-controlled and risk-rated, with evals for bias and prompt-injection resistance?
12. What are our options on data residency and encryption keys?

Question 4 best separates a well-built model from a naive one.
