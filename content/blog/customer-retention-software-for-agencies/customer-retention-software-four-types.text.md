**Customer retention software: four types, one decision**

1. **Type 1: Enterprise CS and retention platform.** Use it when: you run a large self-serve or usage-metered customer base and need to score in-product behaviour. Tools named: Gainsight, ChurnZero, Totango, Planhat.
2. **Type 2: Conversation-based client intelligence** (built around conversations). Use it when: you lose clients with no warning, and your RAG spreadsheet is out of date by the time anyone reads it. Tools named: Kaizan.
3. **Type 3: Survey or NPS tool.** Use it when: you want a read on how happy clients are, twice a year, nothing more. Tools named: Delighted.
4. **Type 4: CRM health-score add-on.** Use it when: you want one more field on the CRM you already run, not another system. Tools named: HubSpot, Salesforce.

Only one type was built around conversations rather than product data.
