**Client onboarding strategy by tier: one 90-day plan, three levels of attention**

Timeline from day 1 to day 30, day 60 and day 90.

- **Tier 1, Strategic:** senior kickoff, weekly check-ins, mid-point risk review, then fortnightly, ending in a live 90-day review.
- **Tier 2, Core:** templated kickoff, bi-weekly check-ins, ending in a written day-90 summary.
- **Tier 3, Standard:** automated welcome, self-serve, day-30 check-in.

Tier 3: the day-90 milestone folds into the first regular account catch-up, no separate review.
