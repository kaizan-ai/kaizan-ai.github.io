**Client onboarding strategies: three tiers. Same 90 days. Different effort.**

The tier-by-stage matrix to agree before anything else gets built.

| Days | Tier 1: Strategic | Tier 2: Core | Tier 3: Standard |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1-30 | Senior kickoff, full stakeholder map built, weekly check-ins begin | Templated kickoff call, stakeholder list drafted, bi-weekly check-ins begin | Automated welcome sequence, shared plan document, one check-in scheduled |
| 31-60 | Weekly check-ins continue, mid-point risk review with the account director | Bi-weekly check-ins continue, stakeholder list reviewed once | Day-30 check-in happens; account moves to standard cadence |
| 61-90 | Fortnightly check-ins, 90-day business review presented live by the account director | Day-90 review sent as a written summary | Day-90 milestone folds into the first regular account catch-up, no separate review |

The sequence stays the same: discover and align, get live, prove early value.
