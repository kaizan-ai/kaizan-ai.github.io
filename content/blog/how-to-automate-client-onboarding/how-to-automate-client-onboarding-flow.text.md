**Client onboarding automation workflow: seven steps, each with an owner and a clock**

Starts the clock: contract signed in the system of record.

1. **Trigger the workflow the moment the contract is signed.** Owner: Sales or account lead. Time: Instant.
2. **Send the intake form and start collecting assets.** Owner: Account manager or onboarding coordinator. Time: Within minutes.
3. **Schedule the kickoff call without the back-and-forth.** Owner: Whoever runs the call. Time: Same day as signature.
4. **Provision access and tools before day one.** Owner: Operations or IT. Time: 24 to 48 hours.
5. **Confirm scope in writing against the signed contract.** Owner: The account lead. Time: Before the kickoff call.
6. **Run the internal kickoff and assign ownership.** Owner: Delivery or account lead. Time: Within 48 hours.
7. **Set the first 30, 60 and 90-day health check.** Owner: Account lead, Head of Client Service. Time: Scheduled at kickoff.

No single tool does all seven. Most firms connect two or three systems they already own.
