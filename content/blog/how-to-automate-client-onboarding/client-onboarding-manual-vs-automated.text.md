**Manual vs automated client onboarding: the same onboarding, run two ways**

**Manual: lives in someone's head**
- **Email threads:** intake details buried across replies
- **Sticky notes:** chasing access, remembered by one person
- **Spreadsheet checklist:** updated whenever someone remembers

"What do I do first?" asked again with every new client.

**Automated: one signature fires the sequence**
1. Contract signed
2. Intake form + reminder
3. Kickoff booked
4. Access requested
5. Scope confirmed by a person

The step worth keeping partly manual: a person reads the scope before it goes out.

Automate the admin. Keep the kickoff call, the scope conversation and the first escalation human.
