**Customer relationship management: the process in seven steps**

**Setup**
1. **Build record.** Owner: Account Director (or whoever onboards the account)
2. **Map stakeholders.** Owner: Account Director with the Account Manager

**The loop: repeats for the life of the account**

3. **Capture conversations.** Owner: Whoever is in the room
4. **Track commitments.** Owner: The person who made the promise, usually the Account Manager
5. **Review health.** Owner: Head of Client Service, with the Account Manager
6. **Escalate risk.** Owner: The Account Manager flags it, the Head of Client Service decides what happens next

Loop back: steps 3 to 6 repeat.

**Renewal**

7. **Close the loop.** Owner: Account Director and Head of Ops/COO together

Steps 3 to 6 repeat for the life of the account. Steps 1, 2 and 7 sit outside the loop as setup and renewal.
