**Client collaboration software, the two lanes: what clients see, and what stays internal**

**Client lane.** Who sees it: the client. Client portal.

- Progress against plan
- Deliverables awaiting approval
- Reporting and results
- Agreed next steps
- Shared files

**Internal lane (what the client never sees).** Who sees it: your team and leadership. Kaizan.

- Client health
- Risk flags
- Stakeholder coverage
- Time invested and margin
- Renewal and expansion signals

The client lane builds trust. The internal lane protects the account.
