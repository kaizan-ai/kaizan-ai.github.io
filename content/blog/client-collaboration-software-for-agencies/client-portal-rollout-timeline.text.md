**Rolling out a portal to existing clients: six steps, no relaunch**

1. **Pick five accounts.** Owner: Head of Client Service. Time: 1 day. Start with accounts that already chase status by email.
2. **Draw the two lanes.** Owner: Account director. Time: 2 hours per account. Decide what the client sees and what stays internal.
3. **Seed the portal.** Owner: Account team. Time: half a day. Load current files, open approvals and the next three dates before anyone is invited.
4. **Tell the client.** Owner: Account director. Time: 15-minute call plus email. Use the script below.
5. **Name one update owner.** Owner: Account manager. Time: 20 minutes a week. Pull the update from captured activity, not from memory.
6. **Review at 30 days.** Owner: Head of Ops or COO. Time: review point. Check logins by contact, sections opened and requests still arriving by email. Then extend to the next ten accounts.

**Script for the client call**

"From Monday you will have one place for files, approvals and weekly progress. Email still works for anything urgent. You will get a short update every Friday. If a section is not useful, tell us and we will change it."

A mid-relationship rollout needs a plan, an owner and a script.
