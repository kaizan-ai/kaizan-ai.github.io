**Software for consultancies: The five jobs of a consultancy stack**

1. **Win work: CRM and proposals.** Contacts, companies and deals. Examples: HubSpot, Salesforce.
2. **Plan: Resource planning or PSA.** Who is free and who is overbooked. Example: Float.
3. **Deliver: Work management or PSA.** Tasks, timelines and approvals. Example: monday.com.
4. **Bill: PSA, time tracking and invoicing.** Ties time to budgets, then invoices. Examples: Productive, Scoro, +4 more.
5. **Keep: Client success platform** (where revenue leaks). Scores relationship health and says what to do next. Examples: Kaizan, Planhat.

Most firms cover the first four jobs. The fifth, keeping clients, runs on a spreadsheet and memory.
