**Software for consultancies · Five jobs by firm size: Which stack fits a 10, 50 and 200 person consultancy**

| Job | 10 people | 50 people | 200 and above |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Win work | Covered: free or low-cost CRM | Covered | Covered |
| 2. Plan | Not yet | Add now: resource planning and a PSA | Covered |
| 3. Deliver | Covered: your work tracker | Covered | Covered |
| 4. Bill | Covered: time and invoicing tool | Covered: PSA | Covered |
| 5. Keep | Not yet: one person holds the client picture | Process only: a lighter client-health process, because spreadsheet RAG reports begin to fail | Covered: client success platform. Kaizan fits best from 200 to 10,000 employees. |

At 200 people and above, you need all five jobs. No single person knows every account.
