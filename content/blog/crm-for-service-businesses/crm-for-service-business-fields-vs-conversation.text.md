**What a CRM structurally cannot hold: the conversation layer**

**A. What a CRM field captures** (Salesforce, HubSpot)
- Stage
- Amount
- Close date
- Notes: free-text box, most people skip it

**B. What the conversation layer holds** (never reaches a field)
- Tone
- Risk mentions
- Stakeholder silence
- Expansion signals

**Where it shows up**
- "A decision-maker goes quiet for six weeks"
- "A client raises the same concern twice in a month"
- "A 'by the way' at the end of a meeting"

The signal usually exists. It almost never makes the trip into a CRM field, because nothing requires it to.
