**Client relation management vs CRM: what's the difference?**

- **Client relation management:** the discipline of understanding, serving and growing a client relationship
  - Outside the CRM: sentiment, stakeholder coverage, risk signal
  - Software: CRM software. Contacts, deals, contract terms, billing history

**The takeaway:** The software is infrastructure for one slice of the discipline.

Client relation management is the discipline, across however many people and tools touch that client.

CRM is one tool that supports part of it: the record-keeping part.

A business can run a pristine CRM while being genuinely blind to how its clients feel.
