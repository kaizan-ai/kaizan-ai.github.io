**Account management software, what it is and what it is not: Match the problem to the tool type**

| If your problem is | You need | Tools in this guide |
|---|---|---|
| We lose clients without warning | 1. Client intelligence platform | Kaizan |
| Deals and contacts live in the sales pipeline | 2. CRM | Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho CRM, Pipedrive, monday CRM |
| We run a handful of very large strategic accounts | 3. Key account management software | DemandFarm, Kapta |
| We run a SaaS customer success team on product usage | 4. Customer success platform | Gainsight, Planhat |

If a tool’s job stops at the contact record and the deal stage, it’s a CRM.
