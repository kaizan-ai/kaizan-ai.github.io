**Churn prediction on conversation data: Churn prediction vs health scoring**

| | Churn prediction | Health scoring |
|---|---|---|
| Output | One output: a risk score, a probability or a band | Four dimensions: Satisfaction, Activity with stakeholders, Relationship strength, Expansion readiness |
| The question it answers | "Will this account leave?" | "What is happening, and why?" |

Both are fed by the same conversation data: calls, emails and chat.

The best churn prediction is a byproduct of good health scoring, not a replacement for it.
