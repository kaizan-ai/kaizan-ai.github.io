**AgencyAnalytics alternatives, decision flowchart: Reporting tool, client success tool, or both?**

Start here: Is your main question about campaign performance, or about relationship risk?

1. **If your real question is** “How did last month’s campaigns perform, and can I show the client a clean report?”
   **You need** a reporting tool: AgencyAnalytics, Databox, Swydo, DashThis, Whatagraph, Looker Studio.
   **Because** this is a data-visualisation job: pull numbers from ad platforms, format them, send them.
2. **If your real question is** “Is this client relationship healthy, and will I see a problem before the renewal conversation?”
   **You need** a client success / relationship intelligence platform (Kaizan).
   **Because** this is a conversation-analysis job: score sentiment, risk, stakeholder coverage and expansion signals from what is actually said.
3. **If your real question is** “Both. I need a clean client report and I need to stop losing accounts we didn’t see coming.”
   **You need** both, run as separate layers.
   **Because** they answer different questions and rarely duplicate each other’s work. More in the next section.

Start from the question you need answered, then pick the tool.
