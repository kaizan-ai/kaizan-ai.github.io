**The agency lifecycle: Five stages, five software jobs**

1. **Pitch.** What has to happen: new business pipeline, proposals, scoping. Job category: CRM and new business.
2. **Onboard.** What has to happen: contracts, kickoff, first 30 to 90 days live. Job category: onboarding.
3. **Deliver.** What has to happen: tasks, timelines, creative production, approvals. Job category: project and delivery management; creative production and review.
4. **Report.** What has to happen: time, budgets, client-facing reporting. Job category: time and resourcing; finance and invoicing; client reporting.
5. **Renew** (the stage most stack audits skip). What has to happen: relationship health, risk, expansion, the renewal conversation. Job category: client intelligence and retention.

**Underneath every stage**
- **Internal and client communications:** Slack, Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Email
- **AI and integration layer:** connects everything else together

Most stack audits stop at deliver and report. Renew is the stage with the most revenue behind it.
