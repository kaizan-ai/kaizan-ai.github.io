**Digital agency software: The eight jobs, with shortlisted tools**

1. **New business and CRM** (Pitch): HubSpot, Salesforce
2. **Onboarding** (Onboard): A template in your PM tool. Dedicated tool only above about 10 at once.
3. **Project and delivery management** (Deliver): Asana, ClickUp
4. **Creative production and review** (Deliver): Figma, Canva, Adobe CC
5. **Time, resourcing and capacity** (Report): Harvest, Toggl
6. **Finance, billing and invoicing** (Report): QuickBooks, Xero
7. **Client reporting** (Report): AgencyAnalytics, Looker Studio
8. **Client intelligence and retention** (Renew): The job every other category leaves open. Kaizan

Most agencies run six to ten tools across these eight jobs, not one platform.
