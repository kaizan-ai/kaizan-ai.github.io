**Digital agency client management: Three lanes, three owners of data**

| Lane | Owns | Tools | Does not hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| CRM | Deal and contract data | HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive | The content of calls, or sentiment on the last three QBRs |
| Project tool | Task and delivery data | Asana, ClickUp, monday.com | Whether the client is happy with delivery |
| Client intelligence layer | Conversations and relationship health: every call, email and chat message | Kaizan | It does not run delivery or hold the pipeline |

The client intelligence layer reads from the CRM and the project tool. The third lane reads from the other two rather than competing with them.
