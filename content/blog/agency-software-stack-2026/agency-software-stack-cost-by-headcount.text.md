**Monthly cost ranges, USD: What the agency stack costs at 20, 80 and 200 people**

| Headcount | Lean best-of-breed | Mid-range PSA | Heavier, with creative tools |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 people | $150 to $400 | $200 to $600 | $2,200 to $4,500 |
| 80 people | $600 to $1,600 | $800 to $2,300 | $8,800 to $18,000 |
| 200 people | $1,500 to $4,000 | $2,000 to $5,800 | $22,000 to $45,000 |

- **Lean best-of-breed:** free or entry CRM, entry PM tool, accounting software
- **Mid-range PSA:** Productive or Scoro
- **Heavier, with creative tools:** Adobe CC, a proofing layer, a PSA

**Per client:** Kaizan is priced per client, with unlimited users, so it sits outside this chart. Portfolio size sets the bill, not headcount.

Illustrative ranges built from published per-seat and per-tier prices, not a verified spending survey. Indicative list prices from third-party trackers, checked 4th of November 2026.
