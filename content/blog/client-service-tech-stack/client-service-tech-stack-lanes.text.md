**The four jobs in a client service tech stack: four lanes, left to right**

1. **Capture.** Get the conversation into a system. Fireflies, Otter, Fathom.
2. **System of record.** Hold the contact, deal and contract. HubSpot, Salesforce.
3. **Score and health.** Judge whether the relationship is strengthening or weakening. Kaizan, Gainsight, Planhat.
4. **Act and automate.** Do something with the score. Zapier, Make, Asana.

**Lighter strip: report and feedback.** Report and survey. Qualtrics, AgencyAnalytics, Looker Studio.

Kaizan captures, scores and acts, and writes back to the CRM.
