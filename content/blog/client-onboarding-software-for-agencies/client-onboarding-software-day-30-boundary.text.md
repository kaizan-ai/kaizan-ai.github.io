**Onboarding software vs client intelligence: what happens after day 30**

Signed.

**Onboarding software (day 0 to 30 to 90)**

- The question it answers: is this client live yet?
- Time-boxed by design. Once the client is live, its job is done.
- Portal, Tasks, Templates, Timeline
- Tools compared in this guide: Rocketlane, GUIDEcx, Dock, Arrows

**Client intelligence (ongoing): Kaizan**

- The question it answers: is this relationship healthy, and do we know before the client tells us?
- Watches the relationship for the years that follow.
- A decision-maker gone quiet, Sentiment sliding, Expansion opportunity

Complements, not alternatives. Most firms eventually need both.
