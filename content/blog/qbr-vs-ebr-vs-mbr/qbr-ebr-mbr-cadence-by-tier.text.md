**Review cadence by account tier: Which cadence fits which client?**

| Account tier | QBR | EBR | MBR |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tier 1:** Top accounts by revenue or risk, roughly 10 to 20% of accounts | Monthly QBR | EBR once or twice a year | Internal MBR feeding both |
| **Tier 2:** Core retained accounts | Quarterly QBR | EBR only when a renewal risk or expansion signal appears | MBR stays internal |
| **Tier 3:** Smaller or long-tail accounts | QBR quarterly, or twice a year | No EBR unless a growth signal appears | No client-facing MBR |

Tier the book, then set the cadence. A health signal, not the calendar, is usually what moves an account between them.
