**Six steps: Switching or adding: a migration checklist**

1. **Tier the book first.** Don't pick cadences before you've grouped accounts by value and risk.
2. **Set the default cadence per tier.** Use the three-tier model above as a starting point, not a final answer.
3. **Separate the QBR deck from the EBR deck.** They answer different questions for different audiences.
4. **Keep the MBR internal unless a client specifically asks for a monthly cadence.** Don't schedule one preemptively; it adds a meeting nobody requested.
5. **Migrate historic account notes once, not per review type.** Pull the last two or three quarters of context into whatever system holds the record, so the first QBR and the first EBR on a newly tiered account both start from the same history instead of two separate memories.
6. **Review the tiering itself on a cadence.** Accounts move. Revisit tiers at least twice a year, and let a health signal move an account between tiers sooner than that when the evidence says so.
