**QBR vs EBR vs MBR: Which review fits which client**

- **QBR (Quarterly Business Review):** A structured meeting between an agency and a client, held roughly every quarter, that reviews the value delivered against agreed goals, surfaces risks and opportunities, and sets the priorities for the next period.
- **EBR (Executive Business Review):** A strategic, executive-level review, usually annual or semi-annual, focused on business impact and the renewal case rather than quarterly detail.
- **MBR (Monthly Business Review):** A monthly, usually internal, operational review of metrics and delivery.

Three different questions, for three different audiences.
