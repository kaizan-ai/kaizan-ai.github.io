**CRM for advertising agencies: one hierarchy, two objects that cut across it**

**The hierarchy (solid lines: has many)**
- Client: Acme Group
  - Brand: Acme Snacks
    - Campaign: Spring retail launch
    - Campaign: In-store sampling programme
  - Brand: Acme Beverages
    - Campaign: Always-on social

**The two objects that cut across it (dashed lines)**
- **Renewal:** attaches to Client or Brand contract (attaches to Acme Group and Acme Snacks)
- **Stakeholder:** tagged to Brand or Campaign (tagged to Acme Beverages and Always-on social)

Dashed line: the object cross-cuts the hierarchy. Solid line: has many.
