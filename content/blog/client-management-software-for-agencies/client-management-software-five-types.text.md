**Client management software for agencies: five types of client management software**

1. **CRM.** Tracks contacts, companies and deals. Tools: HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho CRM, monday CRM. Where it stops: sees only what someone logs.
2. **Project and work tools.** Plans tasks and shares boards. Tools: Airtable. Where it stops: does not read client conversations.
3. **Client portal.** Shares files, approvals and messages with clients. Tools: SuiteDash, Moxo. Where it stops: shows the client your work, not the client's mood.
4. **Agency operations platform (PSA).** Runs projects, time, budgets and margin. Tools: Teamwork, Productive, Scoro. Where it stops: tracks delivery, not the relationship.
5. **Client success software.** Captures client conversations and scores relationship health. Tools: Kaizan, Planhat (Planhat: built for B2B SaaS). Where it stops: needs conversation data to work.

Pick the type first. Pick the tool second.
