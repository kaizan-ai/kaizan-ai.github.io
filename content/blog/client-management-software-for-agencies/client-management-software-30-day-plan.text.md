**Your first 30 days off the RAG spreadsheet (48 hours in total, 12 hours per phase)**

1. **Frame** (days 1 to 5). Name the one problem, map your stack, pick five pilot accounts, save last quarter's RAG ratings as the baseline, score two or three tools. Done when: you have a one-sentence problem, a stack map and a baseline. Owner: Head of client service and COO. 12 hours.
2. **Select** (days 6 to 10). Run demos on your own data, check which clients allow recording and chat capture, complete the security review, choose a tool. Done when: contract or pilot approved. Owner: COO, account directors. 12 hours.
3. **Connect** (days 11 to 15). Connect email, calendar, meetings, chat and CRM for the five accounts, import 90 days of history, run the tool beside the spreadsheet. Done when: data flows for all five accounts and differences are logged. Owner: COO, account directors. 12 hours.
4. **Prove** (days 16 to 30). Train pilot account managers on one daily workflow, use the output in a real call or QBR, freeze the spreadsheet for pilot accounts, measure, decide. Owner: Head of client service and COO. 12 hours.

**Gate, day 30:** a decision is recorded with reasons.
