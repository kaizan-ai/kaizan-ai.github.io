**QBR agenda and format: The 45-minute QBR, minute by minute**

**Before the clock, Step 0: Pull the real history, not memory.** When: two working days before. Owner: account lead. Time: 30 to 45 minutes.

1. **Open with the headline** (0 to 4 min). Owner: account lead.
2. **Value against client goals** (4 to 18 min). Owner: account lead; senior sponsor adds context.
3. **Risks and blockers** (18 to 28 min). Owner: account lead names it, client responds.
4. **Stakeholder check** (28 to 33 min). Owner: senior sponsor.
5. **Next quarter's priorities** (33 to 41 min). Owner: both sides.
6. **Confirm and close** (41 to 45 min). Owner: account lead.

The clock starts at Step 1. Step 0 happens before the room. The scripts for each step are in the guide.
