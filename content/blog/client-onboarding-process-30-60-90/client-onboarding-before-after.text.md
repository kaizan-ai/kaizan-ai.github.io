**Client onboarding, before and after: from a memory-based spreadsheet to a structured plan**

**Before: a RAG spreadsheet, filled in from memory.** Red, amber and green letters, a few question marks and a few blanks.

**After: the structured 30-60-90 plan**

| Step | Owner | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Run the kickoff call | Account Director | 60 min |
| Build the stakeholder map | Account Director + Account Manager | 2 hrs |
| Baseline the account | Account Director | 1 hr |
| Run the first QBR | Account Director + sponsor | 60 min |

Every step gets an owner and a time, instead of a colour filled in from memory.
