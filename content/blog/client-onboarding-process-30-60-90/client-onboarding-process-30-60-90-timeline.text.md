**The client onboarding process: a 30-60-90 day plan for new clients**

**Week 0: sales handover**

- Sales-to-delivery handover call
- Build the internal account brief
- Confirm the kickoff date, attendee list and agenda

**Days 1 to 30: kickoff, stakeholder map, 30-day check-in**

- Run the kickoff call
- Build the stakeholder map
- Agree the 30-day success criteria, in writing
- 30-day check-in call

**Days 31 to 60: cadence, baseline, 60-day review**

- Establish the recurring contact cadence
- Baseline the account: first client-health read, first risk log
- 60-day review: confirm scope is holding

**Days 61 to 90: first QBR, handover to steady-state**

- Prepare and run the first QBR
- Hand the account from onboarding mode to steady-state
- Close the loop: one change for the next onboarding

From signed contract to steady-state delivery, with an owner and a time against every step.
