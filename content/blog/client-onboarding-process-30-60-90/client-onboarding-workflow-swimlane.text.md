**Client onboarding, who owns each step: days 1 to 30, the client onboarding workflow**

Lanes: Sales lead, Account Director, Account Manager, Head of Client Service.

1. **Sales-to-delivery handover call.** Account Director (incoming) + Sales lead. Week 0.
2. **Send the kickoff agenda and pre-reads.** Account Director. 20 min.
3. **Run the kickoff call.** Account Director. 60 min.
4. Branch point, in parallel:
   - **Build the stakeholder map.** Account Director + Account Manager. 2 hrs.
   - **Connect systems of record.** Account Manager. 2 hrs.
5. Rejoin: **Agree the 30-day success criteria, in writing.** Account Director. 30 min.
6. **First proactive update.** Account Manager. 30 min.
7. **30-day check-in call.** Account Director. 30 min.
8. **Later: hand the account to steady-state.** Account Director + Head of Client Service. Day 90.

Stakeholder map and systems connection run in parallel after the kickoff call, and rejoin at the written success criteria.
