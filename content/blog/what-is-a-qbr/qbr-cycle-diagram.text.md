**The components of a QBR: Every QBR runs the same four steps**

QBR cycle: repeats every review.

1. **Prepare.** Pull the real conversation history, not memory.
2. **Review.** Value against goals, risks, stakeholders.
3. **Agree actions.** Owners and dates.
4. **Follow up.** Close the loop before the next cycle.

Start from the real conversation history, end with owners and dates, and close the loop before the next one.
