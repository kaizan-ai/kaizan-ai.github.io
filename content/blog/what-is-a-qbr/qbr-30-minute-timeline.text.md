**QBR run of show: A 30-minute QBR, start to finish**

1. **Context and headline** (0 to 5 minutes). One slide. Where the relationship stands overall, and the one thing this quarter's review is really about.
2. **Value delivered against goals** (5 to 15 minutes). The evidence, in the client's language, of what moved against what they asked for.
3. **Risks, blockers and stakeholder check** (15 to 23 minutes). Name what's not working, including anything your team flagged before the client did.
4. **Next quarter's priorities** (23 to 28 minutes). Two or three commitments, each with an owner and a date.
5. **Confirm and close** (28 to 30 minutes). Read the actions back. Confirm the next review date before everyone leaves the room.

Set the timeboxes before the meeting starts and hold them.
