**The short answer: Gainsight alternatives for agencies and professional services**

- **Gainsight:** Built around usage data and health scores. Overkill for most agencies; right for large SaaS teams with the headcount and telemetry to feed it.
- **Totango (now Odie):** Built around SuccessBLOCs and an AI churn engine. Still telemetry-first; the rebrand simplified packaging, not the data model.
- **ChurnZero:** Built around in-app engagement. Built for product-led motions, not relationship-led account work.
- **Vitally:** Built around a customer 360. Best-reviewed UX of the group; same telemetry assumption underneath.
- **Planhat:** Built around a unified customer data platform. Closest to agency-friendly, but still assumes a data warehouse behind it.
- **Kaizan:** Built around calls, emails and meetings. Built for firms whose health signal is conversational, not logged in a product.

Five are built around product usage. Kaizan is built around client conversations.
