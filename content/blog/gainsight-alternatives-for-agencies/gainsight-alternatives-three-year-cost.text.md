**Gainsight alternatives, three-year cost: what this actually costs over three years**

Illustrative estimate, not vendor quotes. A 150-employee firm with 40 active client relationships.

- **Enterprise platform (Gainsight-equivalent):** per seat, licensed to the CS or account team using it. Roughly $75,000–$135,000, before any admin headcount cost. Year 1: roughly $25,000–$45,000, based on 5–8 licensed seats at $100–350/user/month plus implementation.
- **Kaizan:** per client covered, unlimited users across the business. Roughly £142,560, flat, no added admin cost. Year 1: £47,520 (40 clients × £99/client/month, Starter tier).

**Read with care:** The enterprise range is in US dollars and Kaizan is in pounds, both as published. Neither is converted, so bar lengths are not a like-for-like comparison.

An enterprise bill grows with headcount. Kaizan's grows with the number of clients served.
