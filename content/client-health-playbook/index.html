THE PROBLEM

Most teams find out a client is unhappy when it's already too late.

It's the most-repeated struggle in conversations with agency and consultancy leaders. The root cause isn't a lack of care - it's a measurement problem.

Monthly, not continuous
A once-a-month RAG rating is a snapshot, not a signal. By the time it's updated, sentiment has already shifted.
Subjective, not evidenced
Status is usually one manager's gut feel from memory - not what was actually said on calls or in email threads.
Manual, not scalable
1:1s, Slack-scanning and Google Alerts work for five accounts. Across a full portfolio, they're impossible.
