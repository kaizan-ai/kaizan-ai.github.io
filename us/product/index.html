AI Helpers

AI Helpers built for client growth,
acting around the clock.

Three packs of AI Helpers, plus your own. Every helper is grounded in your company data: docs, decks, transcripts, Slack, CRM, email. They keep learning from every new conversation, so the output gets more personal to each client’s objectives the longer you run them.

ROI Helpers
Agents that read every piece of work, every brief, deck, recap, deliverable, call, across every client, and tell you where the work itself is leaking value. They benchmark across the book, then suggest exactly what would lift output for that one client.
How it behaves
Helpers compare each client’s work against the patterns your best work follows, pulled live from your docs, decks, transcripts and outcomes. Continuously learning, so the suggestion for Acme on Friday is sharper than the one on Monday.
Relationship Helpers
Agents that map every stakeholder and act on the gaps. They catch silence, dormant champions and thin coverage, and write the warm re-intro before your weekly review.
How it behaves
Trained on your conversations: who replies fast, who goes quiet, what tone your champions actually use. The longer you run it, the more the helpers sound like your best AM at their best moment.
Growth Helpers
Agents that listen for the moment a client says something they didn’t mean as a buying signal, and turn it into a proactive, client-specific suggestion. Not generic upsell. The next right move for that client, this week.
How it behaves
Suggestions are specific to that client’s objectives, history and tone, not a template. Helpers read every conversation across every account, so a cue heard on a Wednesday call shows up as a written-up move on Thursday morning.
Custom Helpers
Spin up a helper grounded in your data and your objective: onboarding QA, exec read-outs, pitch prep, capacity planning. Describe the outcome; Kaizan assembles the agent.
How it behaves
Custom helpers inherit the same private memory: every doc, deck, call and Slack channel you connect. They keep learning from your outcomes, so each run is more personalized to that client’s objectives than the last.
Client Health Model

A self-learning client relationships score, grounded in your data.

The unifying score across every Helper. It uses your data to measure relationship health and client satisfaction, not someone else’s benchmark. It learns from your won pitches, kept clients and lost briefs: what predicts a healthy engagement in your company. Not a biased RAG status. Re-tuned weekly against your data.

C
Client satisfaction
How the client actually feels about the work: tone, effort, intent, pulled from every email, call and review.
A
Activity with stakeholders
Volume, velocity and seniority of two-way contact. Catches drift before the client feels it.
R
Relationship & interaction quality
Who you know, how senior, how warm. Spots thin coverage and dormant champions before the work suffers.
E
Expansion strategy
Where the scope can deepen: capability gaps, brief widenings, exec asks. Pulled from the language clients actually use.
