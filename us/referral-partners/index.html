Questions.

What if they are already talking to you?+

We tell you within five business days if they are already an active opportunity, were in our pipeline in the last ninety days, or are an existing or former client. Nothing is awkward because you checked before you made the introduction.

What if two of us register the same company?+

The first registration we accept governs. Whoever registers the company first keeps the introduction.

What if they do not sign within twelve months?+

The registration lapses. You are free to submit it again, and we treat it as a new introduction.

Do I have to sell anything?+

No. You make the introduction, we run the process. Join the first call if you want to, and skip it if you would rather not.

When exactly do I get paid?+

Quarterly, on revenue we have actually collected, with a statement showing the calculation. Pilot fees are paid within 30 days of the pilot ending.

What if I stop being a partner?+

You still get paid on everyone who has already signed, and on registered introductions that sign inside their twelve-month protection window.

Do I need to be a US company?+

No. Non-US partners complete a Form W-8 instead of a W-9. Fees are paid in US dollars either way.
